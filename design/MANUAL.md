# Pickleball '94 — Instruction Manual

Draft text for a printed SNES instruction booklet. The working copy is a
Claude Doc (https://claude.ai/code/artifact/2080a5f3-9ad7-4050-9eb5-d65d0d2f3988);
this file is its snapshot of 2026-10-16. It describes four players on a Super
Multitap as shipped, ahead of `M4-MP-01`. The rules and controls behind it are
in [PLAYER_GUIDE.md](PLAYER_GUIDE.md).

## Welcome to Pickleball '94!

Grab your paddle and step onto the court! Pickleball '94 is fast, arcade-style pickleball for one to four players. Play singles or doubles, climb a ladder of eight wacky teams, drop into open play at the park, or sharpen your shots against the ball machine. Every match is short, every rally counts, and the Champs are waiting at the top.

### Getting Started

1. Make sure the power switch on your Super Nintendo Entertainment System is OFF.
2. Insert the Pickleball '94 Game Pak firmly into the console.
3. Plug a controller into port 1. For two players, plug a second controller into port 2. For three or four players, plug a Super Multitap into port 2 and connect controllers 2, 3, and 4 to it.
4. Turn the power ON. After a moment the title screen appears, with a CPU demo match playing behind it.

Never insert or remove the Game Pak while the power is on.

### The Title Screen

Press Up or Down on the Control Pad to choose a mode, then press START or B to begin. To bring friends along, each presses START on their own controller: the screen shows **2P JOINED!** (and 3P JOINED! or 4P JOINED!). Press START again to drop out.

## Controls

You move with the Control Pad and hit with B, A, and Y. The Control Pad also aims your shot at the moment you hit it.

### On the Court

| Button | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Control Pad | Move your player. At contact, it aims the shot. |
| B | Standard swing. When you are holding the ball, press B to serve, or hold it to charge a faster serve. |
| A | Drive: a fast, flat shot. A mistimed drive sprays wide. |
| Y | Lob: a slow, high shot over an opponent at the net. Hold the Control Pad away from the net for a dink that drops softly into the opponent's kitchen. |
| START | Pause and resume. |
| X, L, R, SELECT | Not used during play. |

### In the Menus

| Button | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Control Pad Up/Down | Choose a row. |
| Control Pad Left/Right | Change the choice on a row. |
| B or START | Confirm. |
| Y | Go back, or put back the settings you had. |

### Aiming Your Shots

- **Up or Down** sends the ball toward that sideline.
- **Toward the net** hits deep.
- **Away from the net** hits short: a drop shot near the kitchen.

### Timing Your Swing

Your swing hits the ball on its first frame with the ball in reach. Meet the ball close to your body and it goes where you aimed. Reach for it at the edge of your swing and it sprays wide and long, often out. Press the button just before the ball arrives, not while it is still far away.

### Serving

A quick press always serves safely. For a faster serve, hold the button and watch the power bar fill and drain, then let go: the fuller the bar, the faster the serve. Hold on too long, letting go as it drains to the first (white) block, and the serve dumps into the net.

## Game Modes

The title screen offers eight choices, top to bottom:

| Mode | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| SINGLES | You against one CPU opponent, climbing the ladder. |
| DOUBLES | You and a CPU partner against a CPU team. |
| OPEN PLAY | Walk around a park with three courts and wait your turn for a game. |
| COURT | Choose the venue, time of day, and court colours. |
| CAREER | Make up to three players, track their stats, and earn unlocks. |
| OPTIONS | Points to win, difficulty, ball speed, camera, and sound. |
| MODIFIERS | Change the rules: big ball, fast ball, win by 2, lava kitchen. |
| PRACTICE | Hit against a ball machine. |

### Singles and Doubles

After you choose SINGLES or DOUBLES, Player Select appears. Up/Down moves between three rows; Left/Right changes the choice. B moves to the next row and, on the paddle row, starts the match. START confirms everything at once. Y steps back a row.

- **MALE / FEMALE**: the female player wears a ponytail.
- **Colour**: BLUE, RED, GREEN, or GOLD. With two players, 1P chooses first, and 2P cannot take 1P's colour.
- **Paddle**: each paddle has the same total of stat points, spent differently.

| Paddle | Power | Control | Speed | Plays like |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Wood | 2 | 5 | 2 | Late or stretched swings stay closer to the target. |
| Blue | 3 | 3 | 3 | All-round. |
| Red | 5 | 2 | 2 | Faster shots; stretched swings spray further. |
| Gold | 2 | 2 | 5 | Recovers sooner after a swing and moves faster while swinging. |

Each human picks their own colour, in controller order; a colour already taken shows as a dark silhouette. In singles, 2P plays the opponent on the right. In doubles, the teams go by controller, and a CPU fills any empty spot:

| Players | Left team | Right team |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 1P and CPU | CPU team |
| 2 | 1P and CPU | 2P and CPU |
| 3 | 1P and 3P | 2P and CPU |
| 4 | 1P and 3P | 2P and 4P |

### Open Play

Open Play takes you to a park with three courts, a game going on each, just like open play at a real park. Each court is a skill level, marked over it and by its colour:

| Court | Colour | CPU level |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <3.5 | Teal | EASY |
| 3.5–4.5 | Gold | MEDIUM |
| 4.5+ | Red | HARD |

1. Walk with the Control Pad. The paddles on each court's rack are the players waiting; yours is the coloured one.
2. Press B at a rack to put your paddle in the stack. Press B again to take it back.
3. When a game ends, the next paddles go on. When it's your turn the screen shows **YOUR GAME!** The captain of the next team in line partners you, and you play doubles against the team you were drawn with.
4. After the game, B takes you back to the park. START in the park returns to the title.

Walk right along the path to reach the **clubhouse**. Inside, press B at the board on the back wall to read your records, or B at the ball machine to practise. You'll still be called for your game while you're inside. Friends can come along too: 2P partners you in every game, and with a Super Multitap, 3P and 4P take the other side of the net.

### Practice

Practice sets up a ball machine on the far side of the court. Choose your settings, press B, then choose your player and paddle.

| Setting | Choices |
| --- | --- |
| SHOT | DRIVE, LOB, DINK, MIX |
| TARGET | KITCHEN, DEEP, FOREHAND, BACKHAND, AT YOU, RANDOM |
| RATE | SLOW, NORMAL, FAST |
| RETURN TO | ANY, KITCHEN, DEEP, LEFT LINE, RIGHT LINE |

Every return that lands in your RETURN TO zone adds to your streak. Yellow corners mark the zone and blink when you hit it; a return anywhere else ends the streak. Each zone keeps its own best streak. Watch the nozzle: before each ball, it turns toward the side the ball will go. Press START and choose MAIN MENU to leave.

### Court

Court picks where you play and how it looks. Colour changes show on the court behind the menu straight away.

- **VENUE**: PARK, LAKESIDE, SUNSET, DINOSAUR, SPACE, or UNDERSEA. AUTO plays each opponent on their home court.
- **TIME**: DAY, DUSK, or NIGHT. At night the court plays under the lights.
- **Colours**: the court surface, the ground around it, the lines, and the kitchen. DEFAULT uses the venue's own colours; SAME makes the kitchen match the surface.

### Career

Career holds up to three players, so a family or friends can share the cartridge. Pick a slot and press B.

- **NEW PLAYER**: type a name on the on-screen keyboard (DEL or Y erases, END or START finishes), then choose BODY, SKIN, HAIR, SHIRT, HAT, and PADDLE.
- **A player's card** shows wins and losses, aces, shutouts, best streak, longest rally, and unlocks. Opening a card makes that player the one you play as.
- **NO PLAYER** plays without recording anything, handy for a friend borrowing controller 1.
- To delete a player, open their card, hold L and R, and press X.

Hats aren't just for looks. Each one trades a little of one paddle stat for another:

| Hat | Raises | Lowers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| VISOR | Control | Power |
| HEADBAND | Speed | Control |
| TRICORN | Power | Control |
| BANDANA | Power | Speed |

Playing earns new hats and hair colours. A player's card shows the next unlock and how to get it, such as WIN A MATCH or BEAT THE PIRATES. When a match earns something, the result screen shows **UNLOCKED!**

### Modifiers

Modifiers change the rules of your matches. Left/Right switches a row, and they combine:

| Row | Choices | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| BALL | NORMAL, BIG | A bigger ball is easier to reach. |
| SPEED | NORMAL, FAST | A quicker ball. |
| SCORING | FIRST TO, WIN BY 2 | WIN BY 2 plays on until someone leads by two. |
| KITCHEN | NORMAL, LAVA | Step into your own kitchen during a rally and you lose the point: **LAVA!** |

B or START keeps your choices; Y puts back what you had.

## How to Play

Win rallies to score points; the first side to 5 points wins the match. Pickleball '94 plays simplified **Arcade Rules**, shown on the scoreboard.

### The Court

The net divides the court in half. On each side, the **kitchen** (the non-volley zone) is the strip right next to the net. Behind it are two **service boxes**, one on each side of the centre line. Lines are always in.

### The Rules

1. **Rally scoring.** Every rally scores a point, and whoever wins the point serves next.
2. **Serve diagonally** into the opponent's service box. A serve that lands in the kitchen, in the wrong box, out, or in the net loses the point.
3. **Two-bounce rule.** The serve must bounce before it is returned, and the return must bounce before the serving side hits it. After that, you may volley the ball out of the air.
4. **Ending a rally.** A ball that lands out, hits the net, or bounces twice on one side loses the point for the side responsible.
5. **Winning.** The first side to reach the target score wins (5 by default; change it in OPTIONS).

**The kitchen.** You may stand in your kitchen, but you can't volley from it: hit a ball out of the air there and you lose the point (**KITCHEN!**). Let it bounce first, or step back behind the line. With the LAVA modifier, just stepping in loses the point.

### Doubles

Each player covers a lane: you play the bottom half of your side, and your partner plays the top half. Your CPU partner leaves balls in your lane to you. The serving side alternates which partner serves as its score changes, always diagonally, and teammates never swap sides.

### Result Banners

When a rally ends, a banner tells you why.

| Banner | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| OUT! | The shot landed outside the court. |
| NET! | The shot didn't clear the net. |
| KITCHEN! | The serve landed in the kitchen, or a player volleyed from inside their own kitchen. |
| FAULT! | The serve landed in the wrong service box. |
| 2 BOUNCES | The receiving side failed to return the ball. |
| VOLLEY! | The ball was hit before a required bounce. |
| LAVA! | A player stepped into their own kitchen (LAVA modifier). |

After a long rally, a ball that lands just out gets a **close call** replay: a close-up of the line shows the ball landing past it, and by how many inches.

### Pausing and Match End

Press START to pause. Choose **RESUME**, **RESTART** (the same match from 0–0), or **MAIN MENU**, and press B. START or Y also resumes.

When the match ends, press B. If you won, you face the next opponent on the ladder; if you lost, B gives you a rematch. START returns to the title.

## The Game Screen

The scoreboard runs across the top of the screen. Rally results appear in large letters over the court, and prompts in the panel at the bottom. You play on the left side of the court, facing the net.

| Where | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Top left | Your score, labelled **P1** (or your Career player's name). |
| Top centre | Who you're playing (**VS DAD**), **ARCADE RULES**, or **DOUBLES**. Shows **MATCH POINT** when one side is a point from winning. |
| Top right | The other side's score: **CPU**, or **P2** with a second player. |
| Over the court | The result banner (**OUT!**, **NET!** and so on), then who won the point (**P1 POINT**, **CPU POINT**). |
| Bottom panel | Prompts such as **PRESS B TO SERVE** and **CPU TO SERVE**. |

### End of a Match

The result screen shows **YOU WIN!**, **CPU WINS**, or, with two players, **P1 WINS** or **P2 WINS**. Beat the Champs and you're crowned **CHAMPION!** Other messages you may see here:

- **NEW RECORD!**: you beat your best win streak or longest rally. Enter your initials (see Saving Your Progress).
- **UNLOCKED!**: your Career player earned a new hat or hair colour.
- **LADDER CLEARED!**: you beat all eight teams.

### Story Cards

Before a ladder match, your opponent introduces themselves (press B to play). After the match, they have something to say, and when you win, the next opponent is named. Lose a point and you might hear about it: now and then the side that lost it says something under the point banner.

## Options

Options sets up every match. Up/Down picks a row and Left/Right changes it. B or START keeps your choices, which are saved on the Game Pak; Y puts back what you had.

| Option | Choices | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| POINTS | 3, 5, 7, 9, 11, 15, 21 | 5 |
| DIFFICULTY | KID, EASY, MEDIUM, HARD | MEDIUM |
| SOUND | ALL, NO CROWD, OFF | ALL |
| BALL SPEED | 70% to 120% | 100% |
| CAMERA | OFF, 50% to 100% | OFF |

### Difficulty

- **KID**: the slowest ball. You swing automatically when the ball reaches you, so just run into its path, and your serve goes by itself after a moment. The Control Pad still aims, and you can still press the buttons yourself.
- **EASY**: a slow ball and easier opponents.
- **MEDIUM**: the normal game.
- **HARD**: tougher opponents who make fewer mistakes and react faster.

### Sound, Ball Speed, and Camera

**NO CROWD** keeps the sound effects but drops the applause and background crowd. With **ALL**, each venue has its own background: birds at the park, gulls by the lake, crickets at sunset, jungle calls with the dinosaurs, blips in space, and bubbles under the sea.

**BALL SPEED** works with every difficulty and with the FAST modifier.

**CAMERA** makes the court lean toward the ball as it moves, with the scenery sliding in layers. Change it while the title demo plays behind the page to see it live.

## Meet Your Opponents

Eight teams stand between you and the title, each with its own look, style, and home court. In singles you face the team's captain (listed first); in doubles you and a CPU partner take on both players. The intro card before each match shows your place, such as **LADDER 3 OF 8**.

| # | Team | Players | Home court | They say… |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | The Hendersons | Dad and Kiddo | Park | "DAD JOKES INCOMING, SPORT!" |
| 2 | The Snowbirds | Marge and Walt | Lakeside | "WE'VE PLAYED SINCE 1962." |
| 3 | The Gym Bros | Brad and Chad | Dinosaur | "DO YOU EVEN DINK, BRO?" |
| 4 | The Pirates | Pete and Bilge | Lakeside | "ARR! PREPARE TO BE BOARDED!" |
| 5 | The Goth Club | Raven and Vlad | Space | "THE KITCHEN IS OUR CRYPT." |
| 6 | The Surf Crew | Kai and Lani | Undersea | "COWABUNGA, DUDE!" |
| 7 | The Twins | Max and Mia | Sunset | "DOUBLE TROUBLE. READY?" |
| 8 | The Champs | Ace and Jade | Sunset | "THE TITLE STAYS WITH US." |

Win and press B to face the next team straight away; lose and B is a rematch. Beat the Champs to clear the ladder, and it starts over. Your records count every ladder clear.

### How They Play

The higher you climb, the more your opponents play the net. From the Gym Bros on, they move up once the serve and return have bounced and volley the ball out of the air. The Pirates through the Twins play right at the kitchen line. Come to the net yourself, and the smarter teams (the Pirates on) answer with soft drops into your kitchen and dink rallies, just like real players.

In doubles, the stronger teams cover the middle and shift with the ball as a pair. They hit down the middle, between you and your partner, and if you split up, one at the net and one back, they'll go after the one in back.

In a singles ladder match, the captain's partner watches from the stands and cheers when their teammate scores.

## Saving Your Progress

The Pickleball '94 Game Pak has a battery-backed memory that saves automatically at the end of each match and when you leave a menu. There is no need to save by hand. It keeps:

- Your OPTIONS, MODIFIERS, and COURT settings.
- Up to three CAREER players, with their looks, stats, and unlocks.
- Your RECORDS against the CPU.
- Your best PRACTICE streak for each RETURN TO zone.

Do not turn the power off while a match is ending or while you are leaving a menu.

### Records

RECORDS is the last row of the CAREER page. It shows wins and losses in singles and doubles, your current and best win streak, shutouts (wins where the CPU scored nothing), the longest rally (shots in one point, counting the serve), and ladder clears.

Beat your best win streak or your longest rally, and the match ends with **NEW RECORD!** Enter three initials, arcade style: Up and Down pick a letter (hold to scroll), B moves to the next letter, Y goes back, and START keeps what's shown. Your initials appear beside the record until someone beats it.

To erase every record, hold L and R on the records page and press X. **There is no confirmation**, so be sure! Court colours and settings are kept.

## Tips from the Pros

- **Swing early, hit close.** Press the button a moment before the ball arrives. A ball met close to your body goes where you aim; a stretch sprays it out.
- **Mind the bounces.** Let the serve and the return bounce. Volley too soon and you'll see **VOLLEY!**
- **Lob the net-rushers.** When opponents crowd the kitchen line, a Y lob sails over their heads.
- **Keep it low.** Against a team at the net, aim at their feet, or dink into their kitchen (Y with the Control Pad held away from the net).
- **Don't split up in doubles.** Stay level with your partner. Split one-up, one-back, and smart teams pick on the player in back.
- **Pick your paddle.** New to the game? The Wood paddle forgives late swings. Love to attack? Try Red. Always a step behind? Gold gets you there.
- **Charge with care.** A full power bar makes a blistering serve, but hold too long and it finds the net.
- **Practice makes perfect.** Set the ball machine to your weakest shot and chase a streak.

### Booklet Layout

- **Format.** Classic North American SNES booklets are landscape, roughly 5.5 × 3.75 in (approximate; measure a real one before ordering). Page count must be a multiple of 4 for saddle stitching.
- **Draft page plan (24 pages).** Cover; health and safety notice; contents; Welcome; Getting Started; Controls (2 pages, controller diagram); Game Modes overview; Player Select; Open Play (2); Practice; Court; Career (2); Modifiers; How to Play (2); Game Screen; Opponents (2, one team per quarter-page with bust art); Options; Saving and Records; Tips; credits and notes pages; back cover.
- **Screenshot size.** Native frames are 256 × 224. Scale by whole numbers only (2× or 3×, nearest-neighbour) to keep pixels crisp; at 300 dpi a 3× shot prints about 2.5 in wide.
- **Trademarks.** This is an unlicensed homebrew. Leave out the Nintendo Official Seal and any "licensed by Nintendo" wording; name the console in plain text only.

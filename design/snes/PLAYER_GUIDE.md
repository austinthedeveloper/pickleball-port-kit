# Pickleball '94 — Player Guide

*The SNES game's guide, as of its 0.18.1 (a few port notes crept in
since). The Godot port's is `game/README.md`.*

One player against the CPU (singles), or with a CPU partner against two CPUs
(doubles), on a single outdoor court. A second player can join on
controller 2. First to 5 points wins (or another score you set in OPTIONS).

On the title screen, Up/Down chooses **SINGLES**, **DOUBLES**, **OPEN PLAY**,
**COURT**, **CAREER**, **OPTIONS**, **MODIFIERS**, or **PRACTICE**. Pressing Start on controller 2 adds a second player
(`2P JOINED!`); press it again to leave. Start or B on controller 1 opens
Player Select (or the park, or the court, records, options, or modifiers page).

## Open play

**OPEN PLAY** takes you to a park with three courts, a game going on each,
just like open play at a real park. The courts are skill levels, marked
over each court and by its colour: **<3.5** (teal, an EASY game),
**3.5-4.5** (gold, MEDIUM), and **4.5+** (red, HARD). Each team plays at
its own level. On KID, every court is KID.

- Walk with the D-pad. The paddles hanging on a court's rack are the
  players waiting for that court; yours is the coloured one.
- Press **B** at a rack to put your paddle in its stack (B again takes it
  back). Walk past a court to see who's playing.
- When a game ends, everyone comes off and the next paddles go on. When
  it's yours: **YOUR GAME!** The captain of the team waiting next at that
  court partners you ("WITH PETE VS SURF CREW"); you play doubles against the
  team you were drawn with, at that court's level, with your POINTS and
  MODIFIERS.
- **Two players:** press Start on controller 2 (on the title or in the
  park) and 2P comes along as your partner in your games; press it again
  to leave.
- After the game, **B** takes you back to the park. **Start** in the park
  goes back to the title.
- **The clubhouse:** walk right along the path and the park scrolls on to
  the clubhouse. Go in through the door at the bottom:
  - **B** at the board on the back wall reads your records, a page per
    press.
  - **B** at the ball machine starts a practice session on your PRACTICE
    settings; **PARK** on the pause menu brings you back.
  - The teams waiting for a court sit on the bench.
  - You're still called for your game while you're inside.

## Practice

**PRACTICE** sets up a ball machine on the far side of the court:

- **SHOT**: DRIVE, LOB, DINK, or MIX.
- **TARGET**: the KITCHEN, DEEP, your FOREHAND (the lower side), your
  BACKHAND, AT YOU, or RANDOM.
- **RATE**: how quickly the next ball comes (SLOW, NORMAL, FAST).
- **RETURN TO**: where your returns must land to count: ANY (anywhere in),
  the KITCHEN, DEEP (near the machine's baseline), or the LEFT LINE or
  RIGHT LINE (your left and right as you face the net). Yellow corners
  mark the zone on the court and blink when you hit it; a return anywhere
  else ends the streak. Each zone keeps its own best streak.

Press B, choose your player and paddle, and the machine starts feeding. Every
return that lands in adds to your streak (P1 on the scoreboard), and your
best streak is saved. Watch the nozzle: before each ball it turns toward
the side it will go to (up for the far sideline, down for the near one,
straight for the middle), and on AT YOU it follows you. Ball speed and the KID swing assist follow your
OPTIONS difficulty, and a BIG or FAST ball from MODIFIERS works here too.
Start pauses; choose MAIN MENU to leave.

## Pause

Start pauses a match or practice. Up/Down picks **RESUME**, **RESTART**
(the same match from 0-0, or a fresh practice session), or **MAIN MENU**,
and B chooses. Start or Y also resumes.

## Options

**POINTS** sets how many points win a match: 3, 5 (the default), 7, 9, 11,
15, or 21. **DIFFICULTY** sets how hard the CPU opponents are:

- **KID**: the slowest ball, and you swing automatically when the ball
  reaches you; just run into its path. Your serve goes by itself after a
  moment too. The D-pad still aims, and you can still press the buttons
  yourself.
- **EASY**: a slow ball and easier opponents.
- **MEDIUM**: the normal game.
- **HARD**: tougher opponents who make fewer mistakes and react faster.

**BALL SPEED** sets how fast the ball moves, from 70% to 120%; 100% is
the standard game. It works with every difficulty (KID and EASY are slower
again) and with the FAST BALL modifier.

**CAMERA** sets how much the court leans toward the ball as it moves, from
50% to 100%, or OFF for a still court. The default is OFF. The scenery
moves with it in layers: the trees and stands behind the court less, the
fence in front more. Change it with
the title demo running behind the page to see it live.

**SOUND** is ALL, NO CROWD (effects without the applause or the
background crowd), or OFF. With ALL, each venue has a quiet background:
birds at the park, gulls and water by the lake, crickets at sunset (and
at night), jungle calls with the dinosaurs, blips in space, and bubbles
under the sea.

B or Start keeps your choices (they're saved); Y puts back what you had.

## Modifiers

**MODIFIERS** changes the rules for your matches. Left/Right switches a row,
and they combine:

- **BALL**: NORMAL or **BIG**, a bigger ball that is easier to reach.
- **SPEED**: NORMAL or **FAST**, a quicker ball. (On KID, FAST is the
  normal speed.)
- **SCORING**: FIRST TO your POINTS, or **WIN BY 2**: at 5-5 in a game to
  5, play on until someone leads by two.
- **KITCHEN**: NORMAL or **LAVA**. Step into your own kitchen (the zone
  next to the net) during a rally and you lose the point: `LAVA!`. The
  kitchen glows on the court behind the page while LAVA is chosen. The CPUs
  know to stay out.

B or Start keeps your choices (they're saved); Y puts back what you had.
The title demo always plays without modifiers.

## Opponents

Against the CPU you climb a ladder of eight opponents, each with their own
look and style: the Hendersons, the Snowbirds, the Gym Bros, the Pirates,
the Goth Club, the Surf Crew, the Twins, and the Champs. In singles you play
the team's captain (the HUD shows `VS DAD`, for example); in doubles you and
a CPU partner play the whole team. Win and press B to face the next opponent
straight away; lose and B is a rematch. After the Champs the
ladder starts over. The records page shows your place.

The higher you climb, the more your opponents play the net: from the Gym
Bros on they move up once the serve and return have bounced and volley
balls out of the air, and the Pirates through the Twins play right at the
kitchen line. Your own CPU partner comes up to volley too, but only when
you're up as well, and it covers the middle: it takes middle balls on its
side and picks off ones it's clearly nearer on yours. Most opponents from
the Gym Bros on cover the middle and shift with the ball as a pair too.
Lob them, or keep the ball low at their feet. Come to the net yourself
and the smarter teams (the Pirates on) answer with soft drops into your
kitchen and dink rallies, as real players do. In doubles they also hit
down the middle, between you and your partner, and the top teams rarely
mis-hit. Split up, one at the net and one back, and they'll go at the one
back.

Before a ladder match, the opponent introduces themselves (press B to
play). After the match they have something to say, and when you win the
next opponent is named. Beat the Champs to clear the ladder: the records page
counts your ladder clears.

## Court colours

**COURT** picks where you play, the time of day, and the colours of the
court surface, the ground around it, and the lines. Up/Down picks a row, Left/Right changes it,
and colour changes show on the court behind the menu straight away. B or
Start keeps your choices, which are saved for every later match; Y puts back
what you had.

- **VENUE**: PARK, LAKESIDE, SUNSET, DINOSAUR, SPACE, or UNDERSEA. AUTO
  plays each opponent at their
  home court. Stop on a venue for a moment and it loads behind the page,
  so you can see it; Y puts the old one back.
- **TIME**: DAY, DUSK, or NIGHT, at any venue. At night the court plays
  under the lights and the crickets come out.
- **KITCHEN** colours the non-volley zone; SAME makes it match the surface.
- **DEFAULT** colours are the venue's own: a teal court with a navy kitchen
  on dirt at the park, green with a blue kitchen on grass by the lake, and
  teal with a clay kitchen on brick at sunset, green with an orange kitchen
  among the dinosaurs, navy with a purple kitchen and cyan lines in space,
  and aqua with a navy kitchen on sand under the sea.

## Quips

Lose a point and you might hear about it: now and then the side that lost
it says something under the point banner. Every ladder team has its own
lines (the pirates say ARRRGH!).

## Career

**CAREER** holds up to three players, so a family or friends can share the
cartridge. Pick a slot and press B:

- **NEW PLAYER** makes one and starts on their name, on a keyboard: move
  to a letter with the D-pad and press B to type it. The empty boxes are
  the places left. DEL (or Y) erases the last letter; END (or Start) is
  done. With nothing left to erase, Y backs out. Then choose their look: BODY, SKIN, HAIR, SHIRT, HAT, and PADDLE
  with Left/Right. The demo player on the court stops at the baseline and
  wears it as you go. B keeps it; Y drops the new player.
- **A player** opens their card: wins and losses, aces, shutouts, best
  streak, longest rally, and the unlocks. Opening a player makes them the
  one you play as. B edits their name and look; Y puts the look back.
- **Gear**: hats aren't just for looks. Each one trades a little of one
  paddle stat for another: VISOR more CONTROL, less POWER; HEADBAND more
  SPEED, less CONTROL; TRICORN and BANDANA more POWER (less CONTROL or
  SPEED). The editor shows the result (PWR, CTL, SPD), and so do Player
  Select's bars. A stat can't go past 5, so a hat does nothing for a
  paddle that already maxes what it raises.
- **Unlocks**: playing earns hats and hair colours. The card shows the next
  one and how to get it (WIN A MATCH for the visor, BEAT THE PIRATES for
  the tricorn, and so on). Locked choices don't show up until earned.
- To delete a player, open their card, hold L and R, and press X.
- **NO PLAYER** plays without a career player: nothing is recorded (for a
  friend borrowing controller 1).

**Playing as your player:** Player Select starts on your player's body,
shirt, and paddle (change them for one match if you like), and your skin,
hair, and hat come along. Your name is on the scoreboard. Open play uses
your player too. When a match earns something, the result shows
**UNLOCKED!** with what you got. Career stats count matches against the
CPU.

## Records

**RECORDS** is the last row of the CAREER page. The cartridge saves your
records against the CPU: wins and losses for
singles and doubles, your current and best win streak, shutouts (wins where
the CPU scored nothing), and the longest rally (shots in one point, the serve
included; 2P matches count for this one too). They update at the end of each
match.

Beat your best win streak or the longest rally and the match ends with
**NEW RECORD!**: enter three initials, arcade style. Up and Down pick the
letter (hold to scroll), B moves to the next, Y goes back, and Start keeps
what's shown. The records page shows them next to BEST STREAK and LONGEST
RALLY (`---` until someone claims them). Once your run holds the best
streak, each win after it raises the record under your initials without
asking again.

On the records table, Start, B, or Y goes back. To erase every record, hold
L and R and press X. There is no confirmation. Court colours are kept.

## Player Select

Up/Down moves between the three rows (MALE/FEMALE, colour, paddle);
Left/Right changes the choice. B moves to the next row and, on the paddle
row, starts the match. Start confirms everything at once. Y steps back a
row, and from the top row returns to the title.

- **MALE / FEMALE**: the female player wears a ponytail.
- **Colour**: BLUE, RED, GREEN, or GOLD. With two players, 1P chooses
  first and 2P cannot take 1P's colour (it shows as a dark silhouette).
- **Paddle**: each paddle has the same total of stat points.

| Paddle | Power | Control | Speed | Plays like |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Wood | 2 | 5 | 2 | Late or stretched swings stay closer to the target. |
| Blue | 3 | 3 | 3 | Standard; the CPU uses it. |
| Red | 5 | 2 | 2 | Faster shots; stretched swings spray further. |
| Gold | 2 | 2 | 5 | Recovers sooner after a swing and moves faster while swinging. |

2P plays the opponent on the right side; in doubles each human gets a CPU
partner. The CPUs take the remaining colours.

## Controls

| Button | Action |
|---|---|
| D-pad | Move. At contact, it also aims the shot. |
| B | Standard swing. When you're holding the ball, press B (or A/Y) to serve, or hold it to charge a faster serve. |
| A | Drive: a faster, flatter shot. Mistimed drives spray wider. |
| Y | Lob: a slow, high shot deep over an opponent at the net. With the D-pad away from the net, it's a dink that lands softly in the opponent's kitchen. |
| X | Slice: slow and low, never deep, and steady off a stretch. It buys time to get back. |
| L or R (hold) | Short: any swing goes short while held (a short shot, a fast drop, a dink, a short slice). The D-pad still moves and aims at a sideline. |
| Start | Open Player Select from the title; pause and unpause during play (in practice too); back to the title from the result screen. |
| Select | Debug and hardware-test ROMs only: OBJ stress scene. |

**Serving:** a quick press always serves safely. For a faster serve, hold
the button and watch the power bar fill and drain, then let go: the fuller
the bar, the faster the serve. Wait too long and let go as it drains to the
first (white) block or below, and the serve dumps into the net. On KID the
serve works as before: one press, or it goes by itself.

**Aiming at contact:** Up/Down sends the ball toward that sideline. Toward the net (Right) hits deep. Away from the net (Left) hits short, a drop shot near the kitchen.

**Timing:** a swing hits on its first frame with the ball in reach. Meet the ball with it close to your body and the shot goes where you aimed. Reach for it at the edge and it sprays wide and long, often out. Press B a moment before the ball arrives, not when it is far away.

## Arcade rules

These are simplified rules; the HUD says **ARCADE RULES**.

- Rally scoring: every rally scores a point, and the point winner serves next.
- First to 5 points wins (change it in OPTIONS). On the result screen, B rematches with the same
  players and Start returns to the title.
- Serve diagonally into the opponent's service box. Landing in the kitchen, in the wrong box, or out loses the point, and so does hitting the net.
- Two-bounce rule: the serve must bounce before it's returned, and the return must bounce before the server's side hits it. After that, volleys are allowed.
- A ball that lands out, hits the net, or bounces twice on a side ends the rally against the responsible side.
- Lines are in.
- No volleys from the kitchen: you can stand in it, but a ball hit out of the air from inside your own kitchen loses the point (KITCHEN!). Let it bounce, or step back first.
- Doubles: each player covers a lane (you play the bottom half of your side,
  your partner the top). Your CPU partner leaves balls in your lane to you;
  the opponents spread their shots across both lanes. The serving side alternates which partner serves
  with its score, always diagonally, and teammates never swap sides.

## Result banners

| Banner | Meaning |
|---|---|
| OUT! | The shot landed outside the court. |
| NET! | The shot didn't clear the net. |
| KITCHEN! | The serve landed in the non-volley zone, or someone volleyed from inside their own kitchen. |
| FAULT! | The serve landed in the wrong service box. |
| 2 BOUNCES | The receiving side failed to return the ball. |
| VOLLEY! | The ball was hit before a required bounce. |

In a singles ladder match, your opponent's doubles partner watches from
the stands and cheers when their teammate scores.

After a long rally, a ball that lands just out gets a **close call**
replay: a close-up of the line shows the ball landing past it, and by how
many inches.

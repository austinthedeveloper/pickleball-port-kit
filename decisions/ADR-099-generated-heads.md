# ADR-099 — New Heads: Eighteen Faces for Opponents and Career Players

Status: **Accepted 2026-10-26**
Milestone: `PORT-102` (the user: "i'd like to work on some new heads and
faces ... The new head/face needs to work with the bodies and hats we
have"; after three rounds of generated art, "This direction works. Write
the ADR and wire the round 3 heads into the game").

## Decision

Eighteen new heads, remaster only, beside the classic long-chinned head
every player has worn so far (which stays, and stays the default):

- **Men** (10): CREW CUT, BEARD, ATHLETE, MOUSTACHE, SHAGGY, GLASSES,
  GOATEE, BALD, STUBBLE, FULL BEARD.
- **Women** (8): BANGS, FOCUSED, GLASSES, LAUGH LINES, LAUGHING, SMILE,
  ROUND SPECS, GRIN.

Player Select keeps the classic head (like ADR-095's patterns, it stays
simple), and the ROM is untouched.

- **Art.** Generated by an image model from `art/faces-r1/PROMPTS_R3.md`
  (rounds 1 and 2 went for caricature and were dropped; `REVIEW.md`),
  then snapped to the game's pixels and its palette roles by
  `art/faces-r1/snap.py`. Each is 48 rows of roles in
  `art/levels/tools/heads.json` (`export_heads.py`); `players.py` stamps
  it at the neck of every pose, unrotated, like the classic head. A head
  uses only the outline, white, skin and hair roles, so it takes every
  skin, hair colour and GLOW.
- **Sheets.** A head can't be a plain overlay on the body sheet (the
  classic head is drawn into it, and a smaller head can't erase it), so
  the build adds the body **without a head** (`nohead`, and
  `nohead_<pattern>` for each pattern) and each head as an overlay on it
  (`head_<id>`). `PlayerLook.head` names one, and
  `PlayerLook.body_texture` composes the two at load, once per
  body, pattern and head. The classic head loads the same body sheets as
  before. Every screen that draws a look (matches, the park, story and
  trip scenes, the watcher, the career card, the hat museum) calls
  `body_texture`/`hat_texture` instead of loading the paths.
- **Hats fit every head.** A hat overlay is now the pixels the hat shows
  (the build's canvas records which part drew each pixel), not the frame
  diffed against the bare one: a diff drops any hat pixel that happens
  to match what's under it, which over another head shows through. Over
  the classic head the hats compose to exactly the same pixels as before.
  The round 3 heads were drawn to the hats' dome, so none pokes out of
  any of the twelve.
- **Who wears what** (`opponents.json`, `story.json`, `trip.json`
  `head`; absent is the classic head): DAD a MOUSTACHE, KIDDO LAUGHING;
  MARGE LAUGH LINES, WALT BALD; BRAD ATHLETE, CHAD CREW CUT; PETE a FULL
  BEARD, BILGE STUBBLE; RAVEN FOCUSED, VLAD a GOATEE; KAI SHAGGY, LANI a
  SMILE; ACE a BEARD, JADE BANGS; PEPPER a GRIN; the ROAD HOGS' BUCK a
  FULL BEARD, DOREEN ROUND SPECS. The TWINS keep the classic head (they
  look alike), and so does Dale Dawson, the man on the box. ROAD TRIP's
  locals roll one (the classic head among them) from the stop's seed.
- **Career players** pick one on EDIT's new HEAD row, under BODY; all are
  free (looks only, no gear). The list is per body, so changing BODY puts
  the head back to CLASSIC. `career.json` `heads` names them.
- **The save.** `Player.head` is a new key (0, CLASSIC, when missing), so
  the layout stays v6.

The bible's career looks (§5.5: 4 skins, 10 hair colours, 13 hats) are
the ROM's and stay true; the heads are the remaster's, as the shirt
patterns are (ADR-095).

## Not done

- Online and the lobby show the Player Select look (KNOWN_ISSUES #54), so
  no heads there.
- No unlocks for heads, and no men's heads on the women's body (or the
  reverse): the women's ponytail is the body's.
- The SNES-sized 32 px sets (`art/sprites/`) have no new heads.

## Consequences

- 44 more role sheets per build (26 headless bodies, 18 heads; about
  0.9 MB in the pack), and the build renders each pose about twice as
  often (about a minute).
- A look with a new head holds one composed texture per body, pattern
  and head for the session (`PlayerLook` caches them).
- `test_career.test_heads` checks every head's sheets, the EDIT row, the
  body change, the save round trip and an older player; `test_view`
  checks that a ladder opponent's composed body differs from the classic
  one only around the head; `test_layout` tries every head on EDIT.

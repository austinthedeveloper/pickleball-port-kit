# New venues: round 1 prompts

The same loop as ROAD TRIP. **Round 1 here:** one concept scene per
venue. Generate them, drop the PNGs in this folder under the names below,
and I review them (`REVIEW.md`). **Round 2:** for the scenes you like, I
write the layer prompts (the concept goes in as the reference). Then I
import them, build the venues and send screenshots.

## The references

- **`ref_scene.png`**: attach this to every concept prompt. It's RUINS in
  play with the HUD painted out. It shows the camera, the court's exact
  place and size, the players' scale and the crowd band.
- **`ref_zones.png`**: where each part goes. You don't need to attach it.
  It's the map I'll cut round 2's layers by.

The prompts are self-contained: paste one block, attach `ref_scene.png`,
and generate at a wide 16:9 size (1920x1080 or the nearest size).

## The shared style (already in every prompt below)

> Keep the reference's exact camera, court position, court size and
> perspective, net, and the four players' size and placement: only the
> setting changes. Warm, rich, detailed pixel-art game style like the
> reference: crisp dark outlines, light from the top left, saturated
> colours. HD remaster, no palette limit. Keep the top-centre sky simple
> (the score panels go there). Spectators stand in one band behind a
> barrier along the back of the court, small like the reference's crowd,
> with faces simple. No text, letters, numbers, logos or brands anywhere.

## Round 2 (after you pick)

For each venue you keep, I'll write these, with its concept as the
reference. You don't need to do anything for them yet.

| Image | What it is |
|---|---|
| `<venue>_backdrop` | The scene above the barrier (sky, buildings, distance), opaque, with no crowd or barrier |
| `<venue>_barrier` | The rail or fence the crowd stands behind, a transparent full-width strip |
| `<venue>_floor` | The surround's material (dirt, tar, planks), a flat seamless top-down texture |
| `<venue>_front` | The near strip's props, transparent, low |
| `<venue>_locals` | 8 spectators, idle and cheering, transparent, in the game's long-chinned face style (with a player sprite as a second reference) |
| `<venue>_extras` | Only where it helps: the tumbleweed, the parrot, the pigeons, the Ferris wheel's lights |

The court, net, players and HUD stay the game's own, so nothing in
round 2 has to match them by eye.
